Enterprise Security

Zero Data Leakage. 100% Isolated Tenants.

We treat your proprietary company data and customer conversations with the highest standard of privacy and security.

🔒 Isolated Vector Tenants: Your files and customer queries are stored in private database containers and never shared across instances.
🚫 No Public LLM Training: Your documents and logs are strictly protected and never used to train public foundation models.
🎯 Deterministic Grounding: The chatbot is mathematically constrained to only cite facts from your whitelisted files.
🛡️ Automated PII Redaction: Personal identifiers, payment card data, and sensitive tokens are masked before logs are archived.
